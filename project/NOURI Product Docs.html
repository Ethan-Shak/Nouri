01 · Product

What NOURI is

NOURI removes a decision most people make three times a day and rarely enjoy making: what to eat. Instead of another menu to browse, NOURI is an agent — it holds your context (health, calendar, budget, preferences), reasons about what you need next, and places the order on your existing delivery apps. You move the dial on how much it does without asking.

The product is built for people for whom eating is a constraint problem, not a craving problem: someone recovering from surgery, a type-2 diabetic managing glucose, an endurance athlete hitting macros, a caregiver feeding a parent, an exec eating in the cracks between meetings. For them, "pick a restaurant" is the wrong primitive. "Keep me fed correctly without my attention" is the right one.

◇
The one-sentence thesis NOURI is the first food app whose success metric is how little you open it. Engagement is a cost, not a goal — the agent earns trust by being right, then asking less.

Three product principles

  • The agent is the interface. The UI exists to make the agent's reasoning legible and its autonomy adjustable — not to give you a catalog to shop.
  • Trust is earned in increments. Users start by approving each order and graduate to silent autonomy as confidence builds. The product never starts fully autonomous.
  • Every decision is explainable. Each order ships with its reasoning chain, the alternatives considered, and a confidence score. Nothing happens in a black box.

03 · Product

Autonomy: the trust dial

The single most important control in the product. It governs only the Execute step — how much human confirmation an order needs before it's placed. It is intentionally a four-rung ladder, not a binary on/off, so trust can be handed over gradually.

○SuggestNOURI proposes options. You tap to order yourself.You decide
◐ApproveNOURI picks one meal. You approve before it places.Confirm each
◑SilentNOURI orders quietly. You get 90 seconds to cancel.90s grace
●Full silentNOURI orders without notifying. Daily digest at 9pm.No interrupts
✶
Design decision Autonomy is derived from the persona at onboarding (e.g. a diabetic defaults to Approve, an athlete to Silent) but always editable in You → Autonomy. New users never land on Full silent — the agent has to earn it.

Defined in AUTONOMY_LEVELS (data.jsx) as an ordered array; the index is the level (0–3), so comparisons like autonomy >= 2 mean "silent or above".

A warm, low-saturation "bone" system. The base is a paper-like off-white, ink is a near-black warm brown, and two muted accents — sage (the agent / positive / on-track) and clay (attention / awaiting you) — carry all the meaning. 09 · Design system

Voice & tone

Calm, editorial, and quietly confident — the voice of a competent assistant who has already handled it. It reassures without being soft, and explains without lecturing.

Do

"Eat well without deciding." · "NOURI is ready." · "We've switched on the sources recommended for you."

Don't

Exclamation marks, hype, emoji as decoration, gamified streaks, "Yay! 🎉", urgency tactics.

  • Lead with the outcome, then the mechanism: "the decision is handled before you feel the hunger."
  • Name the agent as a calm third party ("NOURI picks…", "NOURI hard-blocks…").
  • Numbers are reassurance, not pressure — show the reasoning, never a countdown to FOMO.

11 · Structure

Onboarding flow

An Apple-style setup assistant: one decision per screen, smart defaults, a pinned primary action, and a thin five-step progress bar across the core. It's deliberately quick — the persona template pre-fills most of it, so the later screens are mostly confirmations. 12 · Structure

Onboarding — design decisions

The reasoning behind the choices, so future changes don't accidentally undo them.

Why persona-first, profile-second

Asking a new user to set macro targets and dietary rules cold is high-friction and error-prone. Leading with a situation they recognise ("post-surgery", "athlete") lets one tap seed sensible targets, autonomy, dietary defaults, and recommended connectors. Every later screen then opens pre-filled — the user edits rather than authors. This is what keeps a 7-screen flow feeling "quick".

Why verification is the only hard gate

NOURI will spend the user's money autonomously, so a verified contact is non-negotiable — it's the security root and the channel for approvals/digests. Everything else (profile, connectors, payment) can be deferred or changed later, so only Verify blocks progress. Channel is user's choice (phone or email) to avoid forcing a number on privacy-sensitive users.

✶
OTP UX details that matter Six auto-advancing cells with paste support & backspace-to-previous; masked destination echo (+65 •••• 4567); a 30-second resend countdown; auto-verify on the sixth digit with a verifying → verified transition; and a "wrong details? change" escape hatch. These are the difference between a code screen that feels native and one that feels like a form.

Variants exposed as tweaks

Two directions were left adjustable rather than decided, so they can be A/B'd or chosen by feel:

Gate behavior

For review, the prototype starts at onboarding on every load. Replay onboarding in the Tweaks panel resets the flow at any time (it remounts via an incrementing obKey). In production this becomes a persisted "onboarded" flag with the gate skipped on return.

⚠
Known mock The OTP accepts any six digits and payment fields are non-functional placeholders. These are the two seams where a real backend plugs in — see How to extend.

A small robustness note

Onboarding titles use white-space: nowrap. The phone frame is a fixed 402px wide, so titles never need to wrap — pinning them to one line avoids a web-font reflow artifact (a title measured at one line but painted at two would overlap the body). Keep new titles short enough to fit ~354px of content width.

13 · Build

Code architecture

A single-page React prototype, transpiled in-browser with Babel. No build step. nouri/NOURI.html is the shell — it defines design tokens, loads fonts, declares tweak defaults, and pulls in the scripts in dependency order.

NOURI.htmlShell: tokens, fonts, keyframes, tweak defaults, script order.shell
ios-frame.jsxiOS 26 device bezel, status bar, keyboard, glass pills.chrome
tweaks-panel.jsxuseTweaks() + the Tweaks panel controls & host protocol.chrome
data.jsxDomain data: personas, autonomy, loop steps, connectors, platforms.data
foodart.jsxDeterministic dish art + platform card.data
screens.jsxApp primitives + the four tab screens + sheets/sub-pages.app
ob-kit.jsxOnboarding UI kit (frame, fields, OTP, stepper…).new
onboarding.jsxOnboarding step screens + OnboardingFlow controller.new
app.jsxRoot: the gate, tab/sheet state, scaling, Tweaks wiring.app

The cross-file scope rule

Each <script type="text/babel"> gets its own scope. To share a component or data object across files, assign it to window at the end of the file:

// end of ob-kit.jsx — makes these global to later scripts
Object.assign(window, {
  ObFrame, ObOtp, ObField, ObStepper, ObSwitch,
  ObSelectCard, OB_ALLERGENS, OB_PERSONA_META,
});
⚠
Gotcha Never name a module-level style object const styles = {…} — two files doing that collide and break. Prefix per-file (obPrimaryBtn, btnGhost) or use inline styles.

Rendering & scaling

app.jsx renders a fixed 402 × 874 phone and scales it to the viewport with a CSS transform. The gate is a simple view switch inside the device:

const [view, setView] = useState('onboarding'); // | 'app'
// inside <IOSDevice>:
view === 'onboarding'
  ? <OnboardingFlow key={obKey} onComplete={finishOnboarding} …/>
  : </* TopBar + tabs + BottomNav *//>

15 · Build

How to extend

Add an onboarding step

  1. Build the screen component in onboarding.jsx using ObFrame (pass progress, total, onBack, and a footer button).
  2. Add a case for it in the OnboardingFlow stage switch, wiring onBack / onContinue to the neighbouring stages.
  3. If it should count on the progress bar, add its id to the OB_CORE array and renumber the progress props.

Add a persona

Add a record to PERSONAS and its id to PERSONA_LIST in data.jsx, then give it a glyph + accent in OB_PERSONA_META (ob-kit.jsx). It appears automatically in onboarding and the persona tweak.

Add a new tab / screen

Write the screen in screens.jsx (export it on window), add a tab to the tabs array in BottomNav (app.jsx), and render it in the tab switch. Use an Icon k="…" that exists in the icon set.

Add a tweak

Add the key + default to the EDITMODE block in NOURI.html, then drop a control (TweakRadio, TweakSelect, TweakSlider…) into the TweaksPanel in app.jsx, reading t.key and calling setTweak('key', v).

16 · Build

Roadmap & open questions

Deliberately deferred

  • Sign-in for returning users. "I already have an account" currently routes to the same verify step — a real auth path (and the persisted onboarded flag) is the next gate work.
  • Multi-profile (caregiver). The caregiver persona implies two eaters; the data model is still single-profile.
  • Localisation. Hard-coded to SGD + Singapore restaurants and a +65 default. Currency, market, and platform list need to be market-driven.
  • Notifications & the 9pm digest. Implied by autonomy levels 2–3 but not built.

Open questions worth resolving

  • Should autonomy be per-meal-type (e.g. silent for breakfast, approve for dinner) rather than global?
  • How does the agent handle a rejected order — re-search, ask, or hold? The "Train: less like this" affordance hints at it but the loop-back isn't modelled.
  • Where does grocery / cooking fit, if at all — or is NOURI strictly delivery?
  • What's the trust-building moment that earns an upgrade from Approve → Silent, and should the app prompt it?

This document mirrors the prototype in nouri/NOURI.html. It's meant to be edited as the product evolves — keep the design decisions sections honest about why, not just what.
